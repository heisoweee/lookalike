Questions

Do you store my photos?

No. Your photo is resized on your device, sent once for analysis, and not saved by this site.

Is it free?

Yes, with a small number of free searches per day so it stays available for everyone.

What can I search for?

Almost anything you can photograph: furniture, clothes, shoes, decor, gadgets, plants, accessories.

Some shopping links are affiliate links. If you buy through them, Lookalike may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Photos are sent to an AI provider only to analyse them and are not stored by this site.
